Aloe vera is one of the most versatile ingredients in everyday skin care. It's
light, soothing and easy to work into your daily routine.

## Why aloe vera?

- Lightweight and non-sticky
- Helps skin feel soothed and refreshed
- Works well as part of a simple daily routine

## How to use it

Apply a small amount to clean skin, morning and night. Because it's light, it
layers well under other products.
