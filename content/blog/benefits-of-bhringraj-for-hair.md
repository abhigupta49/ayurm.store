Bhringraj is one of the most loved herbs in traditional Indian hair care. For
generations, families have reached for bhringraj oil as part of their weekly
hair ritual. Here's a simple, honest look at why.

## What is bhringraj?

Bhringraj (sometimes called "false daisy") is a herb traditionally used in
Indian hair care preparations. It is most commonly found infused in hair oils.

## Why people use it

- As part of a relaxing scalp massage
- To leave hair feeling soft and conditioned
- As a comforting, time-honoured weekly ritual

## How to use bhringraj oil

1. Warm a small amount of oil in your palms.
2. Gently massage into the scalp and through the length of your hair.
3. Leave it on for at least 30 minutes, or overnight.
4. Wash off with a gentle shampoo.

## A simple weekly ritual

Oiling your hair once a week can become a calming part of your routine. Pair
bhringraj oil with a gentle shampoo and you have a simple, consistent hair care
habit.
