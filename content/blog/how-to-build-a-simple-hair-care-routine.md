Good hair care doesn't need to be complicated. A simple routine you can stick to
beats a complex one you abandon after a week.

## 1. Oil weekly

Once a week, massage a nourishing oil into your scalp and hair. Leave it on for
30 minutes or overnight, then wash off. Try our
[Bhringraj Hair Oil](/products/bhringraj-hair-oil).

## 2. Cleanse gently

Use a gentle shampoo 2–3 times a week. Avoid over-washing, which can leave hair
feeling stripped. Our
[Amla + Neem Shampoo](/products/amla-neem-anti-hairfall-shampoo) is made for
regular use.

## 3. Be consistent

Consistency matters more than any single product. Pick a routine that fits your
week and stick with it.

That's it — three simple steps for everyday hair care.
